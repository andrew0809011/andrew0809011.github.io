## Why I Started This Blog

Every developer eventually reaches a point where they want to document what they've learned. This is that moment for me.

I built this blog to:

1. **Document my learning journey** — writing forces clarity of thought.
2. **Share useful snippets** — things I keep Googling and want to remember.
3. **Connect with the community** — if something helps even one person, it's worth writing.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Programming & Tools** — languages, frameworks, dev tools I actually use
- **Problem Solving** — real bugs I've fixed and how
- **Side Projects** — things I build for fun

## Let's Go

Thanks for stopping by. If you find something useful, feel free to star the [repo on GitHub](https://github.com/andrew0809011/andrew0809011.github.io).

Stay curious. 🚀
